# JTBD — Jobs To Be Done

## Core Concept
Users "hire" products to get a "job" done. The job is not the product — it's the progress the user wants to make in a specific situation. Focus on the job, not the customer attributes.

## Applicable Scenarios
✅ **Best for**
- Demand mining
- Product positioning
- Competitive replacement analysis

## Key Steps
1. Conduct "switch interview": ask about the moment they decided to hire/fire a product
2. Identify the job statement: "When [situation], I want to [motivation], so I can [outcome]"
3. Classify the job: functional / emotional / social
4. Map the job process (8 steps: define → locate → prepare → confirm → execute → monitor → modify → complete)
5. Identify pain points and opportunities in the job process

## Source
Clayton Christensen, *The Innovator's Solution* (2003); Anthony Ulwick, *What Customers Want*.
