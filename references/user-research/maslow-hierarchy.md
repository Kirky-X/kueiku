# Maslow's Hierarchy of Needs

## Core Concept
5-level need hierarchy: Physiological → Safety → Love/Belonging → Esteem → Self-Actualization. Users are motivated by their current dominant unmet need. Products that address the right need level resonate more deeply.

## Applicable Scenarios
✅ **Best for**
- User need mining
- Product positioning
- Marketing appeal design
- Upgrade path planning

## Key Steps
1. Identify your target users' dominant need level through interviews/behavior analysis
2. Map your product's value proposition to the need hierarchy
3. Assess: does your product address the right need level for your target users?
4. Design marketing appeals that resonate with the dominant need level
5. Plan upgrade paths: as lower needs are met, users seek higher-level fulfillment

## Source
Abraham Maslow, *A Theory of Human Motivation* (1943).
