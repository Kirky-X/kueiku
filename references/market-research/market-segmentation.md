# Market Segmentation

## Core Concept
Divide the market into 3-5 non-overlapping segments based on behavior + JTBD + needs. Each segment should be distinct, measurable, accessible, and actionable. Done early in strategy to clarify who you serve.

## Applicable Scenarios
✅ **Best for**
- Early strategy: defining who to serve
- Resource allocation across segments
- Positioning differentiation

## Key Steps
1. List potential segmentation variables (behavior, demographics, JTBD, needs)
2. Cluster customers into 3-5 segments using the variables
3. Validate: are segments non-overlapping, measurable, accessible, and sizable?
4. For each segment, define: size, growth, willingness to pay, competitive intensity
5. Prioritize segments for targeting
6. Develop segment-specific value propositions
