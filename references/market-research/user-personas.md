# User Personas

## Core Concept
Personas are not decorative fiction like "Zhang San, 35-year-old male who likes sports." Instead, they extract 3 representative personas from real user data, each containing JTBD + Top 3 Pains + Top 3 Gains + One Unexpected Insight, used to remind the team during decision-making "who we are building for."

## Applicable Scenarios
- The team's understanding of "target users" is scattered and inconsistent
- "Users" in PRDs/designs are referred to generically, lacking decision anchors
- Existing personas are limited to demographic labels

## Key Steps
1. Cluster from interview/survey data (reference user-segmentation.md)
2. Select 3 personas (no more than 5, they won't be remembered), each containing:
   - One-sentence identity description ("Independent SaaS founder, 5-person team")
   - JTBD: What Job do they hire the product to complete
   - Top 3 Pains: The 3 most painful specific pain points (with context)
   - Top 3 Gains: The 3 specific expected benefits after using the product
   - One Unexpected Insight: One counter-intuitive insight (makes the persona three-dimensional, avoids template-ization)
3. Each persona paired with 1 real photo (not stock photos)
4. Personas serve as "user proxies" in PRD/design review/roadmap decisions
5. Quarterly review: Check if personas still reflect real users; update if obsolete
