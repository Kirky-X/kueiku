# Technology Adoption Lifecycle

## Core Concept
5-stage model of technology adoption: Innovators (2.5%) → Early Adopters (13.5%) → Early Majority (34%) → Late Majority (34%) → Laggards (16%). The "chasm" between Early Adopters and Early Majority is where most technology products fail.

## Applicable Scenarios
✅ **Best for**
- Technology product marketing
- Crossing the chasm strategy
- Target customer segment selection

## Key Steps
1. Assess current customer composition across 5 adopter categories
2. Identify which stage your product is in
3. If at the chasm (between Early Adopters and Early Majority): develop a whole product solution for a specific beachhead segment
4. Adjust marketing messaging per adopter type
5. Plan strategies to cross the chasm

## Source
Everett Rogers, *Diffusion of Innovations* (1962); Geoffrey Moore, *Crossing the Chasm* (1991).
