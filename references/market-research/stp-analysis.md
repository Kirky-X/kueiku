# STP Analysis

## Core Concept
3-step marketing strategy framework: Segmentation (divide market into segments) → Targeting (select which segments to serve) → Positioning (define how to be perceived in target segments).

## Applicable Scenarios
✅ **Best for**
- Market entry strategy
- Product positioning
- Marketing strategy development

## Key Steps
1. **Segmentation**: Divide the market using demographic, behavioral, or needs-based variables
2. **Targeting**: Evaluate each segment (size, growth, competition, fit); select target segments
3. **Positioning**: Define your positioning in target segments (see Positioning Strategy)
4. Develop marketing mix (4Ps) aligned to STP decisions
5. Validate with market data

## Source
Philip Kotler, *Marketing Management*; foundational marketing framework.
