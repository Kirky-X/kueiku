# Change Management

## Core Concept
Systematically drive organizational change using Kotter's 8-step process + ADKAR model. Kotter focuses on organizational momentum; ADKAR focuses on individual transition. Use both for comprehensive change.

## Applicable Scenarios
✅ **Best for**
- Organizational transformation
- Cultural change
- Large-scale behavior change

## Key Steps (Kotter 8-Step)
1. Create urgency — why change now?
2. Build a guiding coalition — assemble influential change champions
3. Form a strategic vision — clear, compelling future state
4. Enlist a volunteer army — communicate broadly, get buy-in
5. Enable action by removing barriers — remove obstacles
6. Generate short-term wins — celebrate early victories
7. Sustain acceleration — keep momentum, don't declare victory too early
8. Institute change — anchor in culture

## Key Steps (ADKAR per individual)
1. **A**wareness — of the need for change
2. **D**esire — to participate and support
3. **K**nowledge — on how to change
4. **A**bility — to implement new skills/behaviors
5. **R**einforcement — to sustain the change

## Source
John Kotter, *Leading Change* (1996); Jeff Hiatt, ADKAR model (Prosci).
