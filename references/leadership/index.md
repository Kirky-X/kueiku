# Leadership · Leadership

**Applicable Scenarios**: Making judgments on team motivation, talent density, and organizational capability

| Methodology | One-Line Description | Best Scenario | Reference |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Reality Distortion Field** | Using conviction and expression to make the team believe the impossible is possible | Driving teams past self-imposed limitations, selling vision | `reality-distortion-field.md` |
| **A-Player Density** | A-players recruit A-players, B-players recruit C-players — talent density determines the team's ceiling | Hiring decisions, team building, performance management | `a-player-density.md` |

## Minimum Information Requirements for Each Methodology

- **Reality Distortion Field**: Clear goal + team's current belief state + exit conditions
- **A-Player Density**: A-player behavioral standard definition + current team assessment data + hiring/attrition mechanisms

## Routing Trigger Signals

- "Team self-limiting / thinks it can't be done" → Reality Distortion Field (primary)
- "Vision selling / fundraising pitch / rallying speech" → Reality Distortion Field (primary)
- "Hiring standards / declining team quality / talent density" → A-Player Density (primary)
- "Performance management / attrition mechanism / team building" → A-Player Density (primary)

## Common Combinations

- **Team Breakthrough**: A-Player Density (ensure talent density) → Reality Distortion Field (drive breakthrough)
- **Organization Scaling**: A-Player Density (hiring standards) → McKinsey 7S (organizational alignment)
