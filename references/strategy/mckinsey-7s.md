# McKinsey 7S

## Core Concept
7-dimension organizational diagnosis: Strategy, Structure, Systems, Staff, Skills, Style, Shared Values.

## Applicable Scenarios
✅ **Best for**
- Organizational capability assessment, internal consistency check, change resistance analysis

## Key Steps
1. Assess each of the 7 dimensions\n2. Identify misalignments between dimensions\n3. Prioritize changes: start with Shared Values (the center)\n4. Design change plan addressing misalignments\n5. Monitor: do changes in one S cascade to others?

## Source
Tom Peters & Robert Waterman, *In Search of Excellence* (1982); McKinsey & Company.
