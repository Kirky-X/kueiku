# GE-McKinsey Matrix

## Core Concept
Industry attractiveness × business strength 9-cell matrix. An upgraded BCG matrix for diversified portfolio management.

## Applicable Scenarios
✅ **Best for**
- Diversified business portfolio management, SBU resource allocation

## Key Steps
1. List all SBUs (Strategic Business Units)\n2. Assess Industry Attractiveness (High/Medium/Low) for each\n3. Assess Business Strength (High/Medium/Low) for each\n4. Plot on 3×3 matrix\n5. Strategy: Invest/Grow (top-left), Selectivity (diagonal), Harvest/Divest (bottom-right)

## Source
McKinsey & Company / GE (1970s); multi-portfolio analysis.
