# Business Model Canvas

## Core Concept
9-block business model: Key Partners, Key Activities, Key Resources, Value Propositions, Customer Relationships, Channels, Customer Segments, Cost Structure, Revenue Streams. One-page complete business model visualization.

## Applicable Scenarios
✅ **Best for**
- Startup/transformation business model design
- Competitive business model analysis
- Business model pivot

## Key Steps
1. Fill each of the 9 blocks (recommended order: Value Prop → Segments → Channels → Relationships → Revenue → Key Resources → Key Activities → Key Partners → Cost Structure)
2. Validate internal consistency between blocks
3. Identify risks and assumptions per block
4. Compare with competitor business models

## Source
Alexander Osterwalder & Yves Pigneur, *Business Model Generation* (2010).
