# Lean Canvas

## Core Concept
The Business Model Canvas adapted for startups — replaces the 9 modules less relevant to early-stage startups (Key Resources / Key Activities / Key Partners) with fields more focused on "risk and learning" (Problem / Solution / Unfair Advantage), for 1-page rapid business model hypothesis validation.

## Use Cases
- Early-stage startup projects, business model still under validation
- Existing BMC but finding Key Activities and similar modules meaningless for early companies
- Need to align with co-founders in 30 minutes on "what we're betting on"

## Key Steps
1. Problem: List target users' Top 3 pain points + existing alternatives
2. Customer Segments: Target users + early adopters (more specific subset)
3. Unique Value Proposition: One sentence explaining "why you"
4. Solution: Solutions for each Problem (one-to-one mapping)
5. Channels: Channels to reach users
6. Revenue Streams: How to make money
7. Cost Structure: Fixed/Variable costs
8. Key Metrics: Key indicators for business health
9. Unfair Advantage: Advantage difficult to replicate (write "none yet" if absent, force yourself to answer)

## Source
