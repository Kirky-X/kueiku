# Stakeholder Mapping

## Core Concept
Influence×Interest matrix to identify and manage all key stakeholders.

## Applicable Scenarios
✅ **Best for**
- Organizational change, cross-department projects, policy implementation

## Key Steps
1. List all stakeholders\n2. Assess Influence (High/Low) and Interest (High/Low)\n3. Plot on 2×2 matrix\n4. Define engagement strategy per quadrant: Manage Closely (H/H), Keep Satisfied (H-I), Keep Informed (L-H), Monitor (L/L)

## Source
Eden & Ackerman; standard stakeholder analysis methodology.
