# Startup Canvas

## Core Concept
9-block strategy + Cost Structure + Revenue Streams, separating strategy from business model.

## Applicable Scenarios
✅ **Best for**
- Early-stage startups needing to answer strategy and monetization simultaneously

## Key Steps
1. Define strategy blocks (customer, problem, solution, value prop)\n2. Define business model blocks (cost structure, revenue streams)\n3. Validate: does the strategy support the business model?\n4. Iterate strategy and business model independently

## Source
Startup strategy methodology; separating strategic choices from business model design.
