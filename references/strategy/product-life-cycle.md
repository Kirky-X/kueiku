# Product Life Cycle

## Core Concept
4-stage strategy matching: Introduction → Growth → Maturity → Decline.

## Applicable Scenarios
✅ **Best for**
- Product strategy, investment timing, portfolio management

## Key Steps
1. Determine which stage each product is in\n2. Apply stage-appropriate strategy:\n   - Introduction: awareness, early adopters\n   - Growth: market share, differentiation\n   - Maturity: efficiency, defend share\n   - Decline: harvest, divest, or reinvent\n3. Plan transitions between stages

## Source
Theodore Levitt (1965); product life cycle theory.
