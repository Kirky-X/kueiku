# Porter's Five Forces

## Core Concept
Industry competitive structure analysis through 5 forces: Competitive Rivalry, Threat of New Entrants, Bargaining Power of Suppliers, Bargaining Power of Buyers, Threat of Substitutes.

## Applicable Scenarios
✅ **Best for**
- Industry entry assessment
- Competitive strategy formulation
- Industry attractiveness analysis

## Key Steps
1. Assess each of the 5 forces (High/Medium/Low)
2. Identify which forces are strongest (these define industry profitability)
3. Develop strategy to position against the strongest forces
4. Monitor force changes over time

## Source
Michael Porter, *Competitive Strategy* (1980).
