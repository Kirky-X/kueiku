# PESTLE Analysis

## Core Concept
6-dimension macro environment scan: Political, Economic, Social, Technological, Legal, Environmental. Maps the external forces that could impact your strategy.

## Applicable Scenarios
✅ **Best for**
- Entering new markets
- Policy impact analysis
- External risk assessment

⚠️ **When NOT to use**
- As a standalone strategy tool — PESTLE only scans; it cannot rank your options or produce a decision (feed it to SWOT's O/T)
- Single-quarter horizons — macro forces act over years; for near-term risk use FMEA or a risk matrix
- Unbounded brainstorming: without a decision on the table, the scan produces a news digest, not analysis

## Key Steps
1. For each PESTLE dimension, identify key factors affecting your organization
2. Rate each factor: impact (high/medium/low) × certainty (high/medium/low)
3. Prioritize high-impact, high-certainty factors
4. Assess: how do these factors create opportunities or threats?
5. Develop strategic responses for each prioritized factor

## Output Template

```
Scope: [market/geography] × horizon [3-5 yrs] × decision: [what this scan informs]

| Dim | Factor                     | Impact | Certainty | O/T | Response or watch trigger        |
| --- | -------------------------- | ------ | --------- | --- | -------------------------------- |
| P   | [data-localization bills]  | High   | Medium    | T   | [scenario-plan data residency]   |
| E   | [FX swing risk]            | Med    | Low       | T   | [hedge or price clause]          |
| S   | [aging demographics]       | High   | High      | O   | [product line for 55+]           |
| T   | [LLM cost collapse]        | High   | Medium    | O/T | [both: moat review + feature]    |
| L   | [AI labeling regulation]   | Med    | High      | T   | [compliance workstream]          |
| E   | [carbon reporting rules]   | Low    | Medium    | T   | [monitor]                        |

Watchlist (low certainty now): [factors + tripwire signals]
Feeds into: SWOT O/T — dated [today], owner [x], next scan [quarterly]
```

## Failure Modes
- News-digest mode: 25 factors, all medium, nothing prioritized → the impact × certainty cut is mandatory; most factors must die on it
- No decision attached: scan "for awareness" — awareness decays in a week → declare what decision this scan informs before starting
- Set-and-forget: a scan from 2 years ago still circulating → date it, own it, and schedule the refresh

## Evidence Strength
Practitioner consensus — a checklist ensuring breadth of external scanning; it makes no causal claims and has no validation beyond usefulness, and its factor lists are only as good as the research behind them.

## Source
