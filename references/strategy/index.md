# Strategic Analysis

**When to use**: Need to evaluate overall situation, make directional decisions

| Methodology | One-line description | Best scenario | Reference |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **SWOT** | Internal strengths/weaknesses × external opportunities/threats 4-quadrant assessment | Annual planning, product launch, strategic transformation | `swot.md` |
| **PESTLE** | 6-dimension macro environment scan | Entering new markets, policy impact analysis, external risk assessment | `pestle.md` |
| **Porter's Five Forces** | Competitive landscape five forces model | Industry entry assessment, competitive strategy formulation | `porter-five-forces.md` |
| **Business Model Canvas** | 9-block complete business model design and evaluation | Startup/transformation/competitive business model analysis | `business-model-canvas.md` |
| **Stakeholder Mapping** | Influence×Interest matrix, identifying and managing all key stakeholders | Organizational change, cross-department projects, policy implementation | `stakeholder-mapping.md` |
| **Ansoff Matrix** | Existing/new markets × existing/new products 4-quadrant growth strategy | Growth direction selection, market expansion, diversification decisions | `ansoff-matrix.md` |
| **Blue Ocean Strategy** | Value innovation transcending competition, creating uncontested market space | Non-competitive growth, strategic differentiation, red ocean breakthrough | `blue-ocean.md` |
| **McKinsey 7S** | Strategy/Structure/Systems/Staff/Skills/Style/Shared Values 7-dimension organizational diagnosis | Organizational capability assessment, internal consistency check, change resistance analysis | `mckinsey-7s.md` |
| **BCG Matrix** | Market growth rate × relative share 4-quadrant product portfolio management | Product investment allocation, portfolio balance, business trade-offs | `bcg-matrix.md` |
| **Product Strategy Canvas** | 9-block strategy canvas: Vision/Segments/Value Prop/Trade-offs/Can't-Won't | Strategy visualization, challengeable 1-page strategy document | `product-strategy-canvas.md` |
| **Lean Canvas** | BMC's startup-adapted 9 blocks (Problem/Solution/Unfair Advantage) | Early-stage startup 30-minute business model validation | `lean-canvas.md` |
| **Startup Canvas** | 9-block strategy + Cost Structure + Revenue Streams, separating strategy from business model | Early-stage startups needing to answer strategy and monetization simultaneously | `startup-canvas.md` |
| **JDB Value Proposition** | 6-block JTBD template: Who/Why/What before/How/What after/Alternatives | Distilling challengeable value proposition before writing PRD/website | `jdb-value-proposition.md` |
| **Monetization Strategy** | 7 monetization models: Freemium/Subscription/Usage/Seat/One-time/Marketplace/Ads | Business model selection, monetization efficiency optimization | `monetization-strategy.md` |
| **Pricing Strategy** | Van Westendorp PSM + value metric + tier design | Pricing/repricing, price sensitivity assessment | `pricing-strategy.md` |
| **Can't-Won't Defensibility** | Competitor can't or won't moat strategy collection | Investor asks "what if competitors copy you", moat design | `cant-wont-defensibility.md` |
| **Porter Diamond Model** | National competitive advantage 4 factors + 2 variables | Cross-border site selection, industrial policy, national competitiveness analysis | `porter-diamond-model.md` |
| **GE-McKinsey Matrix** | Industry attractiveness × business strength 9-cell matrix (BCG upgrade) | Diversified business portfolio management, SBU resource allocation | `ge-mckinsey-matrix.md` |
| **Strategic Group Mapping** | Intra-industry strategic similarity clusters and mobility barriers | Industry competitive structure analysis, new entrant positioning, strategic differentiation | `strategic-group-mapping.md` |
| **Value Chain Analysis** | Primary + support activities identifying competitive advantage sources step by step | Competitive advantage diagnostics, cost leadership/differentiation strategy, process optimization | `value-chain-analysis.md` |
| **Benchmarking** | Best practice identification against industry/cross-industry benchmarks | Performance improvement, process improvement, best practice learning | `benchmarking.md` |
| **Product Life Cycle** | Introduction/Growth/Maturity/Decline 4-stage strategy matching | Product strategy, investment timing, portfolio management | `product-life-cycle.md` |
| **VRIO Framework** | Value/Rarity/Imitability/Organization 4-question resource-based view | Resource/capability assessment, sustainable competitive advantage identification | `vrio.md` |
| **Wardley Mapping** | Value chain (vertical) × evolution stage (horizontal) strategic terrain map | Strategic evolution awareness, Build/Buy/Outsource decisions, competitive dynamics analysis | `wardley-mapping.md` |

## Minimum Information Requirements per Methodology

- **SWOT / PESTLE**: Requires clear analysis target (product/company/market)
- **Porter's Five Forces**: Requires a named industry + major competitors + per-force competitive evidence
- **Business Model Canvas**: Requires clear analysis target (which company/product)
- **Ansoff Matrix**: Requires clear current product scope and market scope
- **Stakeholder Mapping**: Requires clear project/decision scope
- **Blue Ocean Strategy**: Requires current industry competition factor awareness
- **McKinsey 7S**: Requires clear organization/team scope
- **BCG Matrix**: Requires per-product market growth rate and relative market share data
- **Product Strategy Canvas**: Requires team's preliminary thinking on 9-block strategy
- **Lean Canvas**: Requires early business model assumptions + co-founder alignment
- **Startup Canvas**: Requires strategy thinking + cost/revenue structure data
- **JDB Value Proposition**: Requires clear customer + alternative awareness
- **Monetization Strategy**: Requires product form + unit economics data
- **Pricing Strategy**: Requires reachable users for PSM research + value metric candidates
- **Can't-Won't Defensibility**: Requires competitor awareness + own resource inventory
- **Porter Diamond Model**: Requires target country/industry 4-factor data
- **GE-McKinsey Matrix**: Requires per-SBU industry attractiveness and business strength multi-factor data
- **Strategic Group Mapping**: Requires major industry players' strategic variable data
- **Value Chain Analysis**: Requires per-stage cost and value data + competitor comparison
- **Benchmarking**: Requires accessible benchmark data (at least 2 independent sources)
- **Product Life Cycle**: Requires product sales/competition/customer composition trend data
- **VRIO Framework**: L1: enterprise resource/capability inventory; L2: competitor comparison data
- **Wardley Mapping**: Requires value chain component decomposition + per-component evolution stage judgment + major competitor comparison

- "Business model design/evaluation/restructuring" → Business Model Canvas (primary)
- "Multi-stakeholder alignment / driving organizational change" → Stakeholder Mapping (primary)

## Common Combinations

- **Strategic planning**: PESTLE → SWOT → OKR
  - Step outputs: PESTLE → O/T factor list with evidence; SWOT → 4-quadrant fit assessment consuming that O/T list; OKR → quarterly objectives with measurable key results
  - Branch: policy or regulatory shock surfacing mid-analysis → re-run PESTLE before continuing; no shocks → proceed
  - Final deliverable: macro scan + 4-quadrant assessment + quarterly OKR sheet — one synthesized conclusion, not three stapled reports
- **Business model design**: PESTLE/Porter's → Business Model Canvas → Value Proposition Canvas
- **Competitive analysis**: Porter's Five Forces → SWOT → First Principles
  - Step outputs: Five Forces → industry structure verdict (where profit pools sit and why); SWOT → position fit consuming that structure analysis; First Principles → challenge any "industry law" the plan quietly assumed
  - Branch: analysis target is one company's full assessment → SWOT leads and Five Forces feeds it (see constraint table); industry entry decision → Five Forces leads
  - Final deliverable: industry structure verdict + positioned assessment + assumption audit — synthesized, not stapled
- **Organizational change**: Stakeholder Mapping → Six Thinking Hats → OKR
- **Growth strategy**: Cynefin (judge problem domain) → Ansoff Matrix (select direction) → Blue Ocean (differentiation)
- **Product portfolio optimization**: BCG Matrix (portfolio classification) → Ansoff Matrix (growth direction) → RICE (execution ranking)
- **Organizational capability diagnosis**: McKinsey 7S (internal consistency) → SWOT (internal-external fit) → OKR (improvement goals)
- **Competitive advantage diagnostics**: Value Chain Analysis → Benchmarking → VRIO
- **Diversified portfolio management**: BCG Matrix (initial screening) → GE-McKinsey Matrix (deep assessment) → Ansoff (growth direction)
- **Industry competitive structure analysis**: Porter's Five Forces (overall) → Strategic Group Mapping (intra-industry groups) → Blue Ocean (breakthrough direction)
- **Product stage strategy**: Product Life Cycle (stage positioning) → BCG Matrix (portfolio mix) → OKR (stage goals)
- **Strategic evolution awareness**: Wardley Mapping (value chain × evolution stage) → Blue Ocean (differentiation direction) → Value Chain Analysis (competitive advantage diagnostics)
- **Technology investment strategy**: Wardley Mapping (component evolution positioning) → Gartner Hype Cycle (technology maturity) → DCF (ROI assessment)

## Methodology Mutual Exclusion and Ordering Constraints

| Constraint pair | Rule | Reason |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Lean Canvas ↔ Startup Canvas | **Pick one**, don't use both simultaneously | Output overlaps heavily; both are BMC adaptations for early-stage startups |
| PESTLE → SWOT | **PESTLE before SWOT** | PESTLE expands the O/T dimensions of SWOT; doing PESTLE first avoids external analysis omissions |
| Porter's Five Forces → SWOT | **Porter's before SWOT** (competitive analysis scenario) | Porter's provides industry structure deep analysis, serving as more complete SWOT input |
| Value Chain Analysis → VRIO | **Value Chain first, then VRIO** (competitive advantage diagnostics scenario) | First identify per-stage value sources, then assess whether resources are sustainable |
| SWOT vs Porter's Five Forces | **Choose by analysis target**: comprehensive single-enterprise assessment→SWOT; industry competitive structure→Porter's | SWOT is 4-quadrant comprehensive assessment; Porter's is industry five-forces deep analysis; different dimensions |
