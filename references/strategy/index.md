# Strategic Analysis

**Use cases**: Assessing the overall situation, making directional decisions

| Methodology | One-line description | Best scenario | Reference |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **SWOT** | Four-quadrant assessment of internal strengths/weaknesses × external opportunities/threats | Annual planning, product launch, strategic transformation | `swot.md` |
| **PESTLE** | Six-dimension macro-environment scanning | Entering new markets, policy impact analysis, external risk assessment | `pestle.md` |
| **Porter's Five Forces** | Five-force competitive landscape model | Industry entry assessment, competitive strategy formulation | `porter-five-forces.md` |
| **Business Model Canvas** | 9-module complete business model design and evaluation | Startup/transformation/competitive business model analysis | `business-model-canvas.md` |
| **Stakeholder Mapping** | Influence × interest matrix to identify and manage all key stakeholders | Organizational change, cross-departmental projects, policy implementation | `stakeholder-mapping.md` |
| **Ansoff Matrix** | Four-quadrant growth strategy: existing/new markets × existing/new products | Growth direction selection, market expansion, diversification decisions | `ansoff-matrix.md` |
| **Blue Ocean Strategy** | Value innovation transcends competition, creating uncontested market space | Non-competitive growth, strategic differentiation, red ocean breakthrough | `blue-ocean.md` |
| **McKinsey 7S** | Seven-dimension organizational diagnosis: Strategy/Structure/Systems/Staff/Skills/Style/Shared Values | Organizational capability assessment, internal consistency check, change resistance analysis | `mckinsey-7s.md` |
| **BCG Matrix** | Four-quadrant product portfolio management: market growth rate × relative share | Product investment allocation, portfolio balance, business trade-offs | `bcg-matrix.md` |
| **Product Strategy Canvas** | 9-segment strategic canvas: Vision/Segments/Value Prop/Trade-offs/Can't-Won't | Strategy formalization, a challengeable 1-page strategy document | `product-strategy-canvas.md` |
| **Lean Canvas** | BMC adapted for startups with 9 modules (Problem/Solution/Unfair Advantage) | Early-stage startup business model 30-minute validation | `lean-canvas.md` |
| **Startup Canvas** | 9-segment strategy + Cost Structure + Revenue Streams, separating strategy from business model | Early-stage startups needing to answer both strategy and monetization | `startup-canvas.md` |
| **JDB Value Proposition** | 6-segment JTBD template: Who/Why/What before/How/What after/Alternatives | Crystallizing a challengeable value proposition before writing PRD/website | `jdb-value-proposition.md` |
| **Monetization Strategy** | 7 monetization models: Freemium/Subscription/Usage/Seat/One-time/Marketplace/Ads | Business model selection, monetization efficiency optimization | `monetization-strategy.md` |
| **Pricing Strategy** | Van Westendorp PSM + value metric + tier design | Pricing/price adjustment, price sensitivity measurement | `pricing-strategy.md` |
| **Can't-Won't Defensibility** | Moat strategy set of what competitors can't or won't replicate | Investors asking "what if competitors copy you", moat design | `cant-wont-defensibility.md` |
| **Porter Diamond Model** | Four factors + two contingencies of national competitive advantage | Cross-national site selection, industrial policy, national competitiveness analysis | `porter-diamond-model.md` |
| **GE-McKinsey Matrix** | Industry attractiveness × business strength 9-cell matrix (BCG upgrade) | Diversified business portfolio management, SBU resource allocation | `ge-mckinsey-matrix.md` |
| **Strategic Group Mapping** | Intra-industry clusters of strategically similar firms and mobility barriers | Industry competitive structure analysis, new entrant positioning, strategic differentiation | `strategic-group-mapping.md` |
| **Value Chain Analysis** | Primary + support activities analyzed link by link to identify competitive advantage sources | Competitive advantage diagnosis, cost leadership/differentiation strategy, process optimization | `value-chain-analysis.md` |
| **Benchmarking** | Benchmarking against industry/cross-industry best practices to identify gaps | Performance improvement, process improvement, best practice learning | `benchmarking.md` |
| **Product Life Cycle** | 4-stage matching strategy: Introduction/Growth/Maturity/Decline | Product strategy, investment timing, portfolio management | `product-life-cycle.md` |
| **VRIO Framework** | Four-question resource-based analysis: Value/Rarity/Inimitability/Organization | Resource capability assessment, sustainable competitive advantage identification | `vrio.md` |

## Minimum Information Requirements per Methodology

- **SWOT / PESTLE**: Clear analysis subject (product/company/market)
- **Business Model Canvas**: Clear analysis subject (which company/product)
- **Ansoff Matrix**: Clear current product scope and market scope
- **Stakeholder Mapping**: Clear project/decision scope
- **Blue Ocean Strategy**: Current industry competitive factor awareness
- **McKinsey 7S**: Clear organization/team scope
- **BCG Matrix**: Market growth rate and relative market share data for each product
- **Product Strategy Canvas**: Team's preliminary thinking on 9-segment strategy
- **Lean Canvas**: Early business model hypotheses + co-founder alignment
- **Startup Canvas**: Strategic thinking + cost/revenue structure data
- **JDB Value Proposition**: Clear customer + alternative solution awareness
- **Monetization Strategy**: Product form + unit economics data
- **Pricing Strategy**: Reachable users for PSM research + value metric candidates
- **Can't-Won't Defensibility**: Competitor awareness + own resource inventory
- **Porter Diamond Model**: Four-factor data for target country/industry
- **GE-McKinsey Matrix**: Multi-factor data on industry attractiveness and business strength for each SBU
- **Strategic Group Mapping**: Strategic variable data for major firms in the industry
- **Value Chain Analysis**: Cost and value data for each activity + competitor comparison
- **Benchmarking**: Obtainable benchmark data (at least 2 independent sources)
- **Product Life Cycle**: Product sales/competition/customer composition trend data
- **VRIO Framework**: L1: Enterprise resource/capability inventory; L2: Competitor comparison data

## Common Combinations

- **Strategic planning**: PESTLE → SWOT → OKR
- **Business model design**: PESTLE/Porter's → Business Model Canvas → Value Proposition Canvas
- **Competitive analysis**: Porter's Five Forces → SWOT → First Principles
- **Organizational change**: Stakeholder Mapping → Six Thinking Hats → OKR
- **Growth strategy formulation**: Cynefin (assess problem domain) → Ansoff Matrix (select direction) → Blue Ocean (differentiation)
- **Product portfolio optimization**: BCG Matrix (portfolio classification) → Ansoff Matrix (growth direction) → RICE (execution prioritization)
- **Organizational capability diagnosis**: McKinsey 7S (internal consistency) → SWOT (internal-external fit) → OKR (improvement targets)
- **Competitive advantage diagnosis**: Value Chain Analysis → Benchmarking → VRIO
- **Diversified portfolio management**: BCG Matrix (initial screening) → GE-McKinsey Matrix (deep assessment) → Ansoff (growth direction)
- **Industry competitive structure analysis**: Porter's Five Forces (overall) → Strategic Group Mapping (intra-group) → Blue Ocean (breakthrough direction)
- **Product stage strategy**: Product Life Cycle (position stage) → BCG Matrix (portfolio mix) → OKR (stage targets)
