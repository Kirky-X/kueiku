# Strategic Group Mapping

## Core Concept
Map companies within an industry by strategic similarity. Identify clusters and mobility barriers between groups.

## Applicable Scenarios
✅ **Best for**
- Industry competitive structure analysis, new entrant positioning, strategic differentiation

## Key Steps
1. Identify key strategic dimensions (e.g. price/quality, geographic scope, vertical integration)\n2. Plot all industry players on 2D map\n3. Identify clusters (strategic groups)\n4. Assess mobility barriers between groups\n5. Identify your position and whitespace opportunities

## Source
Michael Porter; strategic group analysis methodology.
