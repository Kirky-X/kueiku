# Benchmarking

## Core Concept
Identify gaps by comparing against industry/cross-industry best practices.

## Applicable Scenarios
✅ **Best for**
- Performance improvement, process improvement, best practice learning

## Key Steps
1. Identify what to benchmark (process, metric, practice)\n2. Select benchmark targets (industry leaders + cross-industry best)\n3. Gather data (at least 2 independent sources)\n4. Analyze gaps: where are you behind? By how much?\n5. Develop improvement plan based on best practices

## Source
Benchmarking methodology; Xerox benchmarking approach (1970s).
