# Product Strategy Canvas

## Core Concept
Use a 9-segment canvas to make "product strategy" explicit as a document that can be challenged, aligned on, and audited. Prevents strategy from staying in the CEO's head or becoming PPT slogans — every field requires falsifiability and decidability.

## Use Cases
- New product/new quarter/new fiscal year strategy formulation
- Team has inconsistent understanding of "what strategy are we actually pursuing"
- Strategy needs to be challenged by board/investors/team

## Key Steps
1. Vision: 3-10 year grand objective (what world is different because of us)
2. Segments: Select target customer segments to serve (explicitly state "who we don't serve")
3. Relative Costs: Cost structure advantages/disadvantages relative to competitors
4. Value Prop: Core value proposition for each segment
5. Trade-offs: Explicitly state "to get A, we give up B" — strategy is about trade-offs
6. Key Metrics: 3-5 leading indicators measuring whether strategy is working
7. Growth: Growth flywheel / Growth channels
8. Capabilities: Key capabilities that must be built
9. Can't-Won't: What competitors can't (Can't) or won't (Won't) do — the moat
