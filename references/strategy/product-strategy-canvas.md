# Product Strategy Canvas

## Core Concept
9-block strategy canvas: Vision, Segments, Value Prop, Trade-offs, Can't-Won't Defensibility, Capabilities, Metrics, Goals, Resources.

## Applicable Scenarios
✅ **Best for**
- Strategy visualization, creating a challengeable 1-page strategy document

## Key Steps
1. Fill each of the 9 blocks\n2. Ensure internal consistency\n3. Validate: can you articulate the strategy in one sentence?\n4. Challenge: would a competitor copy this? Why not?

## Source
Product strategy methodology; adapted from multiple strategy frameworks.
