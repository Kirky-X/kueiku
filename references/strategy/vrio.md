# VRIO Framework

## Core Concept
4-question resource-based view: Is it Valuable? Rare? Costly to Imitate? Organized to capture value? Determines sustainable competitive advantage.

## Applicable Scenarios
✅ **Best for**
- Resource/capability assessment, sustainable competitive advantage identification

## Key Steps
1. List key resources and capabilities\n2. For each, answer 4 questions: Valuable? Rare? Costly to Imitate? Organized?\n3. Classification:\n   - No to V = Competitive Disadvantage\n   - V yes, R no = Competitive Parity\n   - V+R yes, I no = Temporary Advantage\n   - V+R+I yes, O no = Unused Advantage\n   - All yes = Sustainable Competitive Advantage

## Source
Jay Barney (1991); resource-based view of the firm.
