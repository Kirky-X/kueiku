# Blue Ocean Strategy

## Core Concept
Value innovation: simultaneously pursue differentiation and low cost to create uncontested market space.

## Applicable Scenarios
✅ **Best for**
- Non-competitive growth, strategic differentiation, red ocean breakthrough

## Key Steps
1. Map the current competitive landscape (red ocean)\n2. Apply ERRC grid: Eliminate / Reduce / Raise / Create\n3. Design the new value curve\n4. Validate: is the blue ocean commercially viable?\n5. Build the business model to deliver the new value

## Source
W. Chan Kim & Renée Mauborgne, *Blue Ocean Strategy* (2005).
