# Porter Diamond Model

## Core Concept
National competitive advantage: Factor Conditions, Demand Conditions, Related Industries, Firm Strategy/Rivalry + 2 variables (Government, Chance).

## Applicable Scenarios
✅ **Best for**
- Cross-border site selection, industrial policy, national competitiveness analysis

## Key Steps
1. Assess each of the 4 main factors for the target country/industry\n2. Assess Government and Chance variables\n3. Identify competitive advantages from factor combinations\n4. Make location/investment decisions based on diamond analysis

## Source
Michael Porter, *The Competitive Advantage of Nations* (1990).
