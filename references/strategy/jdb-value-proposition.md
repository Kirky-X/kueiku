# JDB Value Proposition

## Core Concept
6-block JTBD template: Who / Why / What before / How / What after / Alternatives. Crystallize a challengeable value proposition.

## Applicable Scenarios
✅ **Best for**
- Distilling a challengeable value proposition before writing PRD or website

## Key Steps
1. Who: who is the customer?\n2. Why: why do they care?\n3. What before: what's their current situation?\n4. How: how does your product help?\n5. What after: what does their life look like after?\n6. Alternatives: what would they do without you?
