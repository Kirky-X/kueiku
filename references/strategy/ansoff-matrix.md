# Ansoff Matrix

## Core Concept
Existing/new markets × existing/new products 4-quadrant growth strategy.

## Applicable Scenarios
✅ **Best for**
- Growth direction selection, market expansion, diversification decisions

## Key Steps
1. Map current products to markets\n2. Identify growth options: Market Penetration (existing/existing), Market Development (existing/new market), Product Development (new/existing market), Diversification (new/new)\n3. Assess risk per option (diversification = highest risk)\n4. Select growth strategy based on risk appetite and capability

## Source
Igor Ansoff (1957); classic growth strategy framework.
