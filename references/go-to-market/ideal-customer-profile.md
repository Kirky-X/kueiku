# Ideal Customer Profile (ICP)

## Core Idea
An ICP is not a vague "target user" but a precise description of the type of customer most likely to successfully sign, retain long-term, and generate positive word-of-mouth. Use a 4-dimension structured definition to prevent sales, marketing, and product teams from each having their own interpretation.

## Applicable Scenarios
- Lead quality is inconsistent, conversion rates are low
- Marketing's "spray and pray" approach keeps CAC high
- Product features get pulled by "long-tail customers," drifting away from core value

## Key Steps
1. Demographics: Observable attributes such as company size/industry/geography/funding stage/tech stack
2. Behaviors: Buying patterns/decision-making process/usage frequency/expansion path
3. JTBD (Jobs to Be Done): What job do they hire the product to accomplish — must be specific
4. Needs: Required features/compliance/integrations for successful product usage
5. Reverse validation: Back into the ICP from your existing top 10% retaining customers, align with and refine the definition above
6. Use the ICP to score leads; both marketing investment and sales prioritization are based on this
