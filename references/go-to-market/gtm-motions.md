# GTM Motions

## Core Concept
7 go-to-market motion types: Inbound (content/SEO), Outbound (sales prospecting), Paid (ads), Community (organic community), Partners (channel partners), ABM (account-based marketing), PLG (product-led growth). Choose the right motion based on ICP, price point, and sales cycle.

## Applicable Scenarios
✅ **Best for**
- GTM motion selection
- Channel strategy design
- Sales/marketing alignment

⚠️ **When NOT to use**
- No ICP or price point defined yet — motion fit cannot be evaluated; define those first (see Ideal Customer Profile)
- Pre-product-market-fit — motions optimize acquisition of demand that doesn't reliably convert yet; validate PMF first
- Evaluating a single channel in isolation — motion choice is a portfolio decision (1 primary + 1 backup), not a beauty contest

## Key Steps
1. Define ICP and price point
2. Assess sales cycle length and complexity
3. Evaluate each GTM motion's fit for your ICP
4. Select 1-2 primary motions + 1 backup
5. Design the motion-specific playbook
6. Measure and optimize per motion

## Output Template

```
Inputs: ICP=[...] price=[...] cycle=[length/complexity]

Motion fit (fit reasons per motion):
  Inbound  [fit + why]   Outbound [fit + why]   Paid [fit + why]
  Community [fit + why]  Partners [fit + why]   ABM [fit + why]   PLG [fit + why]

Selection: primary=[motion] secondary=[motion] backup=[motion]
Why not the rest: [one line each — missing inputs, mismatched cycle, wrong ACV band]
Motion playbook: [stages, owners, per-motion metric]
```

## Failure Modes
- Motion spray: running five motions at low intensity instead of two at full intensity → cap at 2 primary + 1 backup; kill or scale at the review cadence
- Copying a motion without its prerequisites: PLG without a self-serve product, ABM without account data → each motion lists its prerequisites; unmet prerequisite disqualifies
- Measuring motions by activity (posts sent, ads run) instead of pipeline → per-motion metric must be a pipeline/retention metric

## Evidence Strength
Practitioner consensus on the motion taxonomy and fit factors (ACV × cycle × buyer type); motion-level success rates are anecdotal and context-dependent. Treat fit assessments as hypotheses to be instrumented, not conclusions.
