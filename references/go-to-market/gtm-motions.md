# GTM Motions

## Core Concept
7 go-to-market motion types: Inbound (content/SEO), Outbound (sales prospecting), Paid (ads), Community (organic community), Partners (channel partners), ABM (account-based marketing), PLG (product-led growth). Choose the right motion based on ICP, price point, and sales cycle.

## Applicable Scenarios
✅ **Best for**
- GTM motion selection
- Channel strategy design
- Sales/marketing alignment

## Key Steps
1. Define ICP and price point
2. Assess sales cycle length and complexity
3. Evaluate each GTM motion's fit for your ICP
4. Select 1-2 primary motions + 1 backup
5. Design the motion-specific playbook
6. Measure and optimize per motion
