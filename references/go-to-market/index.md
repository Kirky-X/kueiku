# Go-to-Market

**When to use**: Market entry, launch planning, growth flywheel, competitive response, positioning

| Methodology | One-line description | Best scenario | Reference |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Beachhead Segment** | 4 criteria for beachhead: Burning Pain/WTP/Winnable 60-70%/Referral | Crossing the chasm, concentrating on a segment | `beachhead-segment.md` |
| **Ideal Customer Profile** | 4-dimension ICP: Demographics/Behaviors/JTBD/Needs | Sales lead scoring, targeted advertising | `ideal-customer-profile.md` |
| **GTM Motions** | 7 motion types: Inbound/Outbound/Paid/Community/Partners/ABM/PLG | GTM motion selection | `gtm-motions.md` |
| **GTM Strategy** | Channel + messaging + metrics + phased launch | Go-to-market launch plan | `gtm-strategy.md` |
| **Growth Loops** | 5 loop types: Viral/Usage/Collaboration/UGC/Referral | Sustainable growth mechanism design | `growth-loops.md` |
| **Competitive Battlecard** | Where We Win/They Win/Objection/Landmines | Sales competitive response | `competitive-battlecard.md` |
| **Positioning Strategy** | 5 angles × top-5 competitor matrix finding whitespace | User mindshare positioning, differentiated positioning | `positioning-strategy.md` |

## Minimum Information Requirements per Methodology

- **Beachhead Segment**: Requires candidate segment list + 4-criteria evaluation data
- **Ideal Customer Profile**: Requires existing customer data for reverse-engineering or clear assumptions
- **GTM Motions**: Requires ICP + price point + sales cycle awareness
- **GTM Strategy**: Requires GTM Motion selected + channel/metrics/phase planning capability
- **Growth Loops**: Requires product usage data + loop coefficient quantification capability
- **Competitive Battlecard**: Requires competitive product comparison data + sales feedback
- **Positioning Strategy**: Requires top-5 competitor awareness + 5 angles candidates

## Routing Trigger Signals

- "Crossing the chasm / beachhead market selection" → Beachhead Segment (primary)
- "Ideal customer profile definition" → Ideal Customer Profile (primary)
- "GTM motion selection" → GTM Motions (primary)
- "Launch plan" → GTM Strategy (primary)
- "Sustainable growth mechanism / growth flywheel" → Growth Loops (primary)
- "Competitive response / sales battlecard" → Competitive Battlecard (primary)
- "Positioning / user mindshare positioning" → Positioning Strategy (primary)

## Common Combinations

- **Market entry**: Beachhead Segment (pick the winnable wedge) → Positioning Strategy (claim mindshare whitespace) → GTM Strategy (channel + phased launch)
- **Sales enablement**: Ideal Customer Profile (qualify who to target) → GTM Motions (pick the motion matching ICP, price, and cycle) → Competitive Battlecard (arm reps for objections)
- **Positioning refresh**: Competitive Battlecard (win/loss signals) → Positioning Strategy (reposition into the gap competitors left)
