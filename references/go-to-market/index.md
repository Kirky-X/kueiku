# Go-to-Market

**Applicable Scenarios**: Market entry, launch planning, growth flywheels, competitive battlecards, positioning

| Methodology | One-Line Description | Best Scenario | Reference |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Beachhead Segment** | 4 criteria to select a beachhead: Burning Pain/WTP/Winnable 60-70%/Referral | Crossing the chasm, concentrating on winning a segment | `beachhead-segment.md` |
| **Ideal Customer Profile** | 4-dimension ICP: Demographics/Behaviors/JTBD/Needs | Lead scoring, targeted marketing | `ideal-customer-profile.md` |
| **GTM Motions** | 7 motions: Inbound/Outbound/Paid/Community/Partners/ABM/PLG | GTM motion selection | `gtm-motions.md` |
| **GTM Strategy** | Channels + messaging + metrics + phased launch | Launch go-to-market plan | `gtm-strategy.md` |
| **Growth Loops** | 5 flywheel types: Viral/Usage/Collaboration/UGC/Referral | Sustainable growth mechanism design | `growth-loops.md` |
| **Competitive Battlecard** | Where We Win/They Win/Objection/Landmines | Sales competitive battlecards | `competitive-battlecard.md` |
| **Positioning Strategy** | 5 angles × top-5 competitor matrix to find white space | Mindshare positioning, differentiation | `positioning-strategy.md` |

## Minimum Information Requirements for Each Methodology

- **Beachhead Segment**: Requires candidate segment list + 4-criteria evaluation data
- **Ideal Customer Profile**: Requires existing customer data for reverse-engineering or clear assumptions
- **GTM Motions**: Requires ICP + price point + sales cycle understanding
- **GTM Strategy**: Requires selected GTM Motion + channel/metrics/phased planning capability
- **Growth Loops**: Requires product usage data + loop coefficient quantification capability
- **Competitive Battlecard**: Requires competitor product comparison data + sales feedback
- **Positioning Strategy**: Requires top-5 competitor awareness + 5 angle candidates
