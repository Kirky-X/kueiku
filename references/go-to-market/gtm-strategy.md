# GTM Strategy

## Core Concept
Phased go-to-market plan covering: channel selection, messaging framework, success metrics, and launch phases. Translates product strategy into executable market entry.

## Applicable Scenarios
✅ **Best for**
- Launch planning
- Market entry execution
- Cross-functional GTM alignment

## Key Steps
1. Define target segment and ICP
2. Select GTM motions (see GTM Motions)
3. Craft messaging framework: problem → solution → differentiation → proof
4. Define success metrics per phase
5. Plan phased launch: soft launch → general availability → expansion
6. Align cross-functional teams (product, marketing, sales, support)

## Source
Standard GTM methodology; combining multiple GTM frameworks.
