# Competitive Battlecard

## Core Concept
A one-page sales enablement tool with 4 sections: Where We Win / Where They Win / Common Objections & Responses / Landmines (traps to set for competitors). Updated quarterly based on win/loss data.

## Applicable Scenarios
✅ **Best for**
- Sales competitive response
- Win/loss pattern analysis
- Competitive positioning

## Key Steps
1. Gather win/loss data from CRM and sales interviews
2. Identify top 3-5 competitors
3. For each competitor, fill 4 sections: Where We Win / Where They Win / Objections & Responses / Landmines
4. Review with sales team; validate accuracy
5. Distribute and train sales team
6. Update quarterly with new competitive intelligence

## Source
Standard sales enablement methodology; competitive intelligence frameworks.
