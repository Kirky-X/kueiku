# Systematic Research Process

## Core Concept
5-step closed-loop research: Question → Search → Evaluate → Synthesize → Output. Ensures research is thorough, evidence-based, and produces actionable conclusions.

## Applicable Scenarios
✅ **Best for**
- Complex problem research
- Industry research
- Technology selection research

⚠️ **When NOT to use**
- Simple factual lookups — the loop costs more than the answer
- Deadline-driven decisions where "search + first credible source" is the honest ceiling — say so rather than simulating depth
- Research questions you can't state precisely — vague questions generate unbounded search; refine the question first (that's a meta-step the loop presupposes)

## Key Steps
1. **Question**: Define the research question clearly; decompose into sub-questions
2. **Search**: Gather information from multiple source types (academic, industry, expert interviews, data)
3. **Evaluate**: Assess source credibility, cross-validate findings, flag biases
4. **Synthesize**: Integrate findings into a coherent narrative; identify patterns and gaps
5. **Output**: Produce actionable conclusions with evidence, confidence levels, and recommendations

## Output Template

```
Question: [precise, decidable] — sub-questions: 1. [...] 2. [...]
Decision this research serves: [what will change based on the answer]

Search log: [source → type (academic/industry/primary data) → date accessed]
  Sources by type: academic [n] / industry [n] / primary data [n] / expert [n]

Evidence table:
  | Finding | Sources (≥2 independent?) | Confidence (High/Med/Low) | Conflicting evidence |
  | [X]     | [S1, S2]                  | High                      | [S3 disagrees — why] |

Synthesis: [pattern across findings] — remaining gaps: [what we still don't know]
Output: answer to each sub-question + recommendation + confidence + [what would change our mind]
```

## Failure Modes
- Search-ending-at-confirmation: research stops at the first supporting source — the answer was chosen before the search → the evidence table requires ≥2 independent sources per finding, and conflicts must be shown, not smoothed
- Source-stacking without credibility weighting: vendor white papers and peer-reviewed work listed as equals → label source type and incentive per row; vendor claims about themselves get discounted explicitly
- Synthesis ghostwriting: the narrative smuggles in prior beliefs between the evidence rows → every synthesis claim must cite the evidence rows that support it; uncited narrative lines get cut

## Evidence Strength
Mechanism-level — the loop is a process discipline distilled from established research practice (systematic review norms, source triangulation); it has no outcome validation of its own, but its components (pre-registration of questions, multi-source triangulation, explicit confidence) map onto documented bias countermeasures.
