# Product Trio

## Core Concept
Product discovery should be conducted collaboratively by a PM + Designer + Engineer trio, not by the PM alone then handed off. The trio each represents feasibility/usability/viability perspectives, interviewing users together weekly, avoiding "discovery done but engineering can't keep up" or "great UX but high tech debt."

## Applicable Scenarios
✅ **Best for**
- Continuous discovery where discovery is still borne by PM alone
- Engineers/designers don't interact with users
- Need to introduce technical and design constraints during discovery

⚠️ **When NOT to use**
- 3-person startup where everyone already talks to everyone — the structure exists to rewire a broken handoff, not to add meetings
- Highly regulated discovery with legal in every session — the trio becomes a quartet; that's fine, but plan for it
- Teams where weekly user contact is impossible (long sales cycles, rare users) — adapt cadence, or the "weekly" ritual becomes fiction

## Key Steps
1. Form the trio: PM (value), Designer (usability), Engineer (feasibility), clarify each perspective's responsibilities
2. Establish weekly interview cadence: all three participate (can rotate facilitator, but all present)
3. Jointly maintain the Opportunity Solution Tree; all three can add/challenge opportunities
4. Solution ideation and experiment design produced jointly; technical feasibility intervenes during design phase
5. PM makes final decisions, but tech and design have veto power within their domains

## Output Template

```
Trio: PM [name] / Design [name] / Eng [name] — cadence: [weekly, day]

Interview block: [date] — participants [all three? y/n] — user profile [who]
  Signals captured: [opportunities added to tree / assumptions challenged]
  Feasibility note from Eng: [constraint spotted early]
  Usability note from Design: [interaction risk spotted early]

Tree status: opportunities [n] open / [n] covered — oldest open: [x weeks]
Decisions this week: [made jointly? by PM with vetoes noted?]
Health check: is any trio member becoming a passenger? [answer + fix]
```

## Failure Modes
- Trio in name, PM in practice: engineer silently yielding all judgment → enforce the perspective roles: engineer must voice feasibility, designer usability, every session
- Interview spectator mode: all three attend but only the facilitator talks → rotate who leads interviews; observers write notes, not novels
- Cadence collapse: interviews drift to "when there's time", discovery reverts to quarterly → protect the slot on calendars; a missed week is fine, a missed month is the old broken process

## Evidence Strength
Practitioner consensus — coherent with research on cross-functional teams and shared user exposure (decision latency and handoff loss are well-attested failure modes); the specific trio composition and weekly cadence are practice prescriptions, not validated designs, and outcomes depend on organizational support.

## Source
