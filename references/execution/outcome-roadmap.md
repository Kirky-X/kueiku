# Outcome Roadmap

## Core Idea
Traditional feature roadmaps list "next quarter we'll build feature X" — that's output, not outcome. Outcome Roadmap forces every roadmap item to be reframed as "Enable [segment] to [customer outcome] so that [business impact]," making the roadmap verifiable by business results rather than on-time delivery.

## Use Cases
- Roadmap has devolved into a feature checklist, unable to answer "why are we doing this"
- Cross-team debates about roadmap priorities drag on endlessly
- After launch, no one can determine whether it was a "success"

## Key Steps
1. Transform each roadmap item using the template:
   - Enable [target segment]
   - to [specific customer-side outcome]
   - so that [specific business-side impact]
2. Validate:
   - If you can't fill in the customer outcome, it's an internal request and shouldn't be on the roadmap
   - If you can't fill in the business impact, it's not aligned with strategy
3. Prioritize by outcome value, not by feature effort
4. Keep outcome + candidate solutions for each roadmap item (don't lock into a single approach)
5. Post-launch review: Was the customer outcome achieved? Was the business impact realized? If not, iterate or cut it
